About the Lab & Research Scope

The iTech Research Lab is an academic research group certified by the National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (CNPq) and based at the Federal Institute of São Paulo (IFSP). The laboratory develops experimental and applied investigations in embedded technologies, distributed systems, and artificial intelligence, focusing on assistive tools and automation.

Assistive & Inclusive Tech

Smart devices, cognitive monitoring systems, accessibility tools, and digital solutions for health informatics.

IoT & Sensor Networks

Telemetry systems, embedded microcontrollers, RFID tracking, and real-time distributed data acquisition.

Applied AI & Modeling

Machine learning workflows, biological signal processing, and intelligent systems for education and science.
